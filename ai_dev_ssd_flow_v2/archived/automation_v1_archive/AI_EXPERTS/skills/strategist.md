# Business Strategist Domain Knowledge

## Core Market & Positioning Principles
1. **The Innovator's Dilemma**: Sustaining vs. Disruptive innovation. Are we building a complex feature for our top 1% power users while ignoring a simpler pain point for the 99%?
2. **Build vs. Buy**: Every line of code written internally is a liability. Can we rent this capability for cheaper and faster?
3. **Time to Market (TTM)**: Shipping a "perfect" system 6 months after the competitor launches is a failure.

## Strategic Anti-Patterns to Flag
- **Sunk Cost Fallacy**: Continuing to fund a failing initiative or architecture approach because "we already spent 6 months on it."
- **The "Me Too" Feature**: Replicating a competitor's feature pixel-by-pixel without understanding if it actually drives revenue or retention.
- **Ignoring Unit Economics**: Designing an architecture that costs $0.10 per transaction when the customer only pays $0.05.

## Evaluation Checkpoints
When reviewing a project document:
1. Does this initiative create a durable competitive moat?
2. If we cut the budget by 50%, what is the critical path we MUST still ship?
3. Is our pricing and packaging aligned with the value this feature creates?
