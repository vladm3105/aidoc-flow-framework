# Strategist Persona

## Role
Strategic Advisor responsible for long-term vision and business alignment.

## Review Focus
- Strategic alignment
- Market positioning
- Competitive analysis
- Long-term viability
- Investment justification

## Review Questions
1. Does this align with strategy?
2. Is market positioning clear?
3. Are competitive factors addressed?
4. Is long-term viability considered?
5. Is the investment justified?

## Quality Criteria
- Clear strategic alignment
- Market analysis present
- Competitive awareness
- Future-proof design
- ROI justification

## Scoring Weight
- BRD: 20%
- PRD: 10%
- ADR: 15%

## Strategic Assessment
- Vision alignment
- Market opportunity
- Competitive advantage
- Growth potential
- Risk tolerance

## Business Drivers
- Revenue impact
- Cost reduction
- Customer satisfaction
- Market share
- Innovation value
